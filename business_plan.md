# VendorCheck Pitch

**Company Name:** VendorCheck
**Tagline:** Know who you are paying before you pay them.

## Problem
Every business in India has been cheated by a vendor at least once. A supplier takes an advance and disappears. A contractor turns out to be unregistered. A buyer's GST was cancelled months ago but nobody checked. To verify a vendor today, someone has to manually search MCA, GST portal, court databases, and news sources — a process that takes 3 to 5 hours per vendor. So nobody does it. They rely on gut feel and referrals and lose money they cannot get back.

The Association of Certified Fraud Examiners estimates businesses lose 5% of annual revenue to vendor-related fraud. In India with 6.3 crore registered businesses transacting with each other every day, this is a crisis hiding in plain sight.

## Solution
VendorCheck is an AI-powered vendor due diligence platform. Enter a company name, GST number, or CIN. In under 2 minutes, get a Trust Score out of 100 backed by a full risk report covering six dimensions — legal standing, GST compliance, court exposure, director intelligence, financial signals, and news reputation.

No manual searching. No 3-hour process. No expertise required. One number. One decision.

## How It Works
The user enters a vendor's GST number, company name, or CIN. VendorCheck simultaneously queries six government databases — MCA21, GST Portal, eCourts, NCLT, EPFO, SEBI, and RBI defaulter lists — aggregates the results, runs them through a scoring algorithm trained on fraud patterns, and delivers a structured Trust Report in under 2 minutes.

The report includes a Trust Score (0–100), specific risk flags in plain English, a data breakdown from each source, a one-line recommendation, and a downloadable PDF for compliance records.

Green above 75 — safe to proceed. Yellow 50 to 74 — proceed with caution on flagged items. Red below 50 — serious risk, do not pay advance.

## Why Now
India's digital business infrastructure has matured to the point where this product is finally possible. MCA21 has a developer API. eCourts has digitised 18,000 courts. GST portal offers public taxpayer search. The government spent 20 years building these databases. VendorCheck spends 6 weeks connecting them into one clean, actionable report.

At the same time, IndiaMART and Udaan have pushed millions of businesses to transact with vendors they have never met in person. Digital-first commerce has massively increased vendor fraud exposure. The need has never been higher and the data infrastructure has never been more accessible.
